---
title: "Matching engine"
description: "How the venue matches orders off chain and settles each fill on chain"
---

Numo matches orders off chain and settles every fill on chain. The order book lives in
markets-service. Each fill is submitted to `Matching.verifyAndMatch` on Base, which checks both
signatures and moves the assets.

## How a match happens

1. You submit a signed order to [`POST /v1/orders`](/api-reference/endpoint/create). It rests on
   the book.
2. The matcher polls the book several times a second. On each pass it takes the best crossed pair
   in each market, in [price-time priority](/matching/order-book).
3. The fill size is the smaller of the two remaining amounts. The fill price is the maker's limit
   price.
4. The execution service submits the fill on chain. Your order's filled amount updates once the
   transaction settles.

A market handles one fill at a time. A large order fills against resting orders one by one.

## Maker and taker

The order created later is the taker on each fill. The earlier one is the maker. If both were
created at the same instant, the one with the higher `order_id` is the taker. See
[Fees](/matching/fees) for what this means for what you pay.

## When a fill would revert

The matcher simulates each fill before it is sent. If the chain would refuse it, both orders stay on
the book.

- **A refusal that depends on balances** (for example, an account short of margin) is retried with
  backoff. Nothing is cancelled.
- **A refusal that cannot change** while both orders stay as they are skips that pair. Each order can
  still fill against other orders. An order refused this way against three different subaccounts is
  cancelled with `cancel_reason: "repeated_permanent_revert"`.
- **An order that can never settle as signed** is cancelled with
  `cancel_reason: "unmatchable_signed_order"`.

## Futures

The matcher skips a futures series while it is closed on chain: before it opens, after
`last_trade_time`, or while its manager is paused. Before each futures fill it also checks:

- The fill price is inside the [fill band](/futures#the-fill-band).
- Each account adding risk has enough initial margin. See
  [Pre-trade margin check](/risk/margin-math#pre-trade-margin-check).
