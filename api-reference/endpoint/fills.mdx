title: 'List fills'
openapi: 'GET /fills'
---

Your fills, newest first. Use them to reconcile balances. [Order history](/api-reference/endpoint/order-history) shows the same activity per order.

Send the same `X-Numo-Auth` frame as order history. The owner comes from the signature.

## Reading a fill

- `liquidity` is `taker` if your order crossed the book, `maker` if it rested.
- `side`, `price` and `size` are your order's side, the price in USDC per unit of the base, and the size.
- `fee` is what you paid in USDC, `0` for a maker. It is absent when unknown.
- `tx_hash` is the Base transaction that settled the fill. Older fills may not have one.
- `spot_contract` restates a spot fill at display scales, and `balance_delta` is what it moved.
- A trade between two of your own subaccounts is listed once per order, with one `trade_id`.

## Variation margin

Futures only. `mark_price` is the mark when the fill's transaction ran. `vm_usdc` is what settled to your account, negative when paid: your side × the size in the base × (`mark_price` − `price`). The retired `USDC-cNGN-30NOV26` reports `vm_cngn` instead.

## Retention and paging

Fills are kept indefinitely. Pages hold up to `limit` fills (default 50, maximum 100). Pass `next_before` as `before` for older fills. The last page has no `next_before`.
