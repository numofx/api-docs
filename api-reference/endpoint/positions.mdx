An account's futures positions and margin, read from chain. No authentication is needed.

## Reading a position

`futures` has one entry per series the account holds.

- `contracts` is signed. A long gains when the base strengthens against the dollar.
- `notional_usdc` is |`contracts`| × the contract size × `mark_price`.
- `status` is `trading`, then `closing` after `last_trade_time`, then `settled`.
- `pending_vm_usdc` is variation margin accrued since the last settlement, positive when owed to you. See [Variation margin](/futures#variation-margin). The retired `USDC-cNGN-30NOV26` reports `pending_vm_cngn` instead.

## Margin

`futures_account` reports margin once for the whole account:

- Below zero `im_surplus_usd`, the account cannot open or add to a position.
- Below zero `mm_surplus_usd`, the account is liquidated.

## Unreadable fields

A value the venue could not read is left out. Treat an absent field as unknown, not as zero.
