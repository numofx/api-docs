---
title: 'Get positions'
openapi: 'GET /positions'
---

Returns an account's futures positions in every listed series, and its margin under the
MarginEngine, read from chain. No authentication is needed, because positions are public on chain.

## Reading a position

Each entry under `futures` is one series the account holds. A series it is flat in is not listed.

- `contracts` is signed; `side` is `long` when it is positive. A long gains when the base strengthens
  against the dollar.
- `notional_usdc` is the size in USDC at the mark: |`contracts`| × the contract size × `mark_price`.
- `status` is `trading` before `last_trade_time`, `closing` from then until the settlement price is
  fixed, and `settled` after. A closing series still holds its position.
- `mark_price` is the series' mark in USDC per unit of the base, and `mark_updated_at` is when it was set.

## Pending variation margin

Variation margin accrues at each mark and is realised into cash at each settlement.
`pending_vm_usdc` is what has accrued since the account was last settled, in USDC, positive when
owed to you. The retired `USDC-cNGN-30NOV26` reports `pending_vm_cngn` instead, in cNGN. It runs from each fill's own price, so it is not simply the mark's move × your
position. See [Variation margin](/futures#variation-margin).

## Margin

Margin covers the whole account, including every series and the cNGN it holds, so it is reported
once, under `futures_account`, as `im_surplus_usd` and `mm_surplus_usd`. Below zero on initial
margin, the account cannot open or add to a position. Below zero on maintenance margin, the account
is liquidated. `futures_account.manager` names the engine the account is under, and only series under that engine
count toward its margin. `futures_account` is absent for an account under no futures engine.

## Unreadable fields

A value the venue could not read from chain is left out, and the rest of the response is still
served. Treat an absent field as unknown, not as zero. An entry without `contracts` is a series
whose position could not be read.
