---
title: 'List order history'
openapi: 'GET /orders'
---

Your orders in every status, newest first: `active`, `matching`, `filled`, `cancelled` and `expired`.

## Authentication

Sign this exact message with `personal_sign`, using unix seconds and no trailing newline:

```text
<domain> wants you to view your Numo order history.
Address: <address>
Nonce: <nonce>
Issued At: <issued_at>
Expiration Time: <expiry>
```

Send `address`, `signature`, `nonce`, `issued_at` and `expiry` as base64url-encoded JSON in the `X-Numo-Auth` header. A frame can be reused until it expires, up to 24 hours. WebSocket frames are not accepted here.

## What traded

`filled_quote` is the USDC that actually changed hands. Use it to reconcile, not the order's amount × limit price, which overstates a marketable order. It is absent when nothing filled.

## Retention and paging

Orders that traded are kept indefinitely. Cancelled and expired orders with no fills are pruned after 30 days.

Pages hold up to `limit` orders (default 50, maximum 100). Pass `next_before` as `before` for older orders. The last page has no `next_before`.
