---
title: "Index price"
description: "How the cNGN index is built from off-chain naira rates and published on chain"
---

The index is the reference rate for cNGN against USDC. It centres the futures
[fill band](/futures#the-fill-band), bounds the [mark](/markets/mark-price-and-bounds), sets the
settlement price, and values cNGN held as [collateral](/collateral).

## Sources

1 cNGN redeems for 1 naira, so the index tracks the naira's rate against the dollar. It reads three
NGN-per-USDT rates:

- Quidax (`usdtngn`)
- Textile (`USDT_NGN`)
- Bybit P2P

USDT is treated as equal to USDC.

## How it is computed

1. **Sample.** Every minute, the venue reads all three sources and takes the median. A sample is
   discarded if any source is missing, or if any source is more than 1.5% from the median.
2. **Average.** Every minute, it publishes the time-weighted average of the last five minutes of
   samples. It needs at least three samples, the newest no older than three minutes.
3. **Publish.** It signs the average into the index feed on Base. A single update can move the
   index by at most 1.5%.

### Peg check

The index also watches cNGN's own peg: the 15-minute average of cNGN's price in naira on Quidax. If
cNGN trades more than 1% away from 1 naira, no sample is taken and the index stops updating.

## Staleness

The index feed has a 20-minute heartbeat. If it has not updated in 20 minutes, it is stale:

- `band_status` on [`GET /v1/markets`](/api-reference/endpoint/get) reads `index_stale`.
- The chain refuses every futures fill, and the venue refuses new futures orders.
- The mark does not update.

Trading resumes as soon as the index updates.

## Direction

On chain, the index feed is in USDC per cNGN, with 18 decimals. The futures market quotes cNGN per
USDC, so `index_price` on `GET /v1/markets` is the inverse.

| Where | Unit | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Index feed on chain | USDC per cNGN | `0.000725` |
| `index_price` on a future | cNGN per USDC | `1380` |

The index feed is `0xFaC420d160C7c219A72DC676971670980bAC20a5` on Base.

## Spot

The `cNGN-USDC` spot market does not use the index. Spot prices come only from its order book.
