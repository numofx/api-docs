<Update label="2026-10-08" description="Market identifiers">
  **The markets are identified as `cNGN-USDC` and `cNGN-PERP`.** These are the values `market`,
  `symbol` and `ticker_id` carry everywhere, matching `display_name`. `GET /v1/markets` and
  `GET /v1/integrations/tickers` list the canonical identifiers only. Every response and websocket
  frame names the market by its canonical identifier, whichever identifier the request used.

  **The old identifiers are deprecated aliases until 2027-01-06.** `USDCcNGN-SPOT` and
  `USDCcNGN-PERP` still resolve, by exact match, wherever an identifier is read: `symbol`,
  `market` and `ticker_id`, including websocket subscribe and unsubscribe. A request that uses one
  returns the same data as the canonical request plus two headers: `Deprecation: true` and
  `X-Canonical-Market: <canonical identifier>`. On 2027-01-06 the aliases are removed and such
  requests return `400 unknown_market`.

  **An unknown or missing market is now a 400.** `GET /v1/book`, `GET /v1/trades` and
  `GET /v1/candles` return `{"error": "unknown_market", "message": …, "markets": [...]}` when the
  `symbol` or `asset_address` + `sub_id` names no listed market (`no such market: <identifier>`),
  or when the request names no market at all (`symbol or asset_address is required`). Before this
  change both cases silently returned the spot book, so a client that sent a wrong identifier read
  spot prices and never saw an error. See [Errors](/errors#instrument).

  Unchanged: `display_name`, `order_entry_spec` (`cngn_usdc_spot_v1` and `cngn_usdc_perp_v1`),
  every address, and the `asset_address` + `sub_id` form of naming a market.
</Update>
