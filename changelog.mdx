<Update label="2026-10-19" description="Futures positions on the API">
  **`GET /v1/positions` serves futures positions.** Pass `subaccount_id`; no signature is needed.
  `futures` lists the account's position in every listed series it holds: `market`, `sub_id`,
  `status` (`trading`, `closing` or `settled`), signed `contracts`, `side`, `notional_usdc`,
  `mark_price` and `mark_updated_at`, and `pending_vm_cngn`, the variation margin accrued since the
  account was last settled. `futures_account` gives the account's MarginEngine margin once, as
  `im_surplus_usd` and `mm_surplus_usd`. A value the venue could not read is left out rather than
  shown as zero. See [Get positions](/api-reference/endpoint/positions) and
  [Futures market](/futures#read-a-position).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-16" description="cNGN futures and the MarginEngine">
  **Trading accounts are under the MarginEngine.** `margin_manager_address` on `cNGN-USDC` is now the
  MarginEngine, `0xA19E296021c9DF25b90D96C2A0793CC3D0800758`, which went live on 2026-10-13. `POST /v1/deposits`
  opens new accounts under it, so `managerForNewAccount` must name it. Withdrawals take the cash
  asset `0xA74E49b4Ed7cb176bc02ef4D8a1A3240C9aD4272` and the cNGN escrow
  `0x37c976bb5d4887a714ef19AF6B83e34fe2f37c98`. The retired wrapped assets are still accepted for
  older accounts.

  **A cash-settled cNGN future is listed: `USDC-cNGN-30NOV26`.** It is priced in cNGN per USDC and
  sized in USDC of notional, 1,000 a contract, and expires on 2026-11-30 at 14:00 UTC.
  `GET /v1/markets` describes it with `amount_scale`, `fill_band_enforced`, `fill_band_low`,
  `fill_band_high` and a `future` object. `POST /v1/orders` refuses an order outside the fill band
  or from an account not under the MarginEngine with a **422**, an order past the last trade time
  with a **409**, and an order it cannot check with a **503**. `reduce_only` is accepted on futures.
  See [Futures market](/futures).

  **The perpetual is no longer documented.** The perp guide and the `GET /v1/positions` reference
  are removed. Read futures positions on chain, as [Futures market](/futures#read-a-position)
  describes.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-08" description="Market identifiers">
  **The markets are identified as `cNGN-USDC` and `cNGN-PERP`.** These are the values `market`,
  `symbol` and `ticker_id` carry everywhere, matching `display_name`. `GET /v1/markets` and
  `GET /v1/integrations/tickers` list the canonical identifiers only. Every response and websocket
  frame names the market by its canonical identifier, whichever identifier the request used.

  **The old identifiers are deprecated aliases until 2027-01-06.** `USDCcNGN-SPOT` and
  `USDCcNGN-PERP` still resolve, by exact match, wherever an identifier is read: `symbol`,
  `market` and `ticker_id`, including websocket subscribe and unsubscribe. A request that uses one
  returns the same data as the canonical request plus two headers: `Deprecation: true` and
  `X-Canonical-Market: <canonical identifier>`. On 2027-01-06 the aliases are removed and such
  requests return `400 unknown_market`.

  **An unknown or missing market is now a 400.** `GET /v1/book`, `GET /v1/trades` and
  `GET /v1/candles` return `{"error": "unknown_market", "message": …, "markets": [...]}` when the
  `symbol` or `asset_address` + `sub_id` names no listed market (`no such market: <identifier>`),
  or when the request names no market at all (`symbol or asset_address is required`). Before this
  change both cases silently returned the spot book, so a client that sent a wrong identifier read
  spot prices and never saw an error. See [Errors](/errors#instrument).

  Unchanged: `display_name`, `order_entry_spec` (`cngn_usdc_spot_v1` and `cngn_usdc_perp_v1`),
  every address, and the `asset_address` + `sub_id` form of naming a market.
</Update>
