---
title: "Mark price"
description: "Where the futures mark comes from, how often it moves, and what it drives"
---

The mark price is the price the MarginEngine values a futures position at. It drives
[variation margin](/futures#variation-margin), [margin](/risk/margin-math) and
[liquidation](/risk/liquidation). Spot has no mark.

## Where it comes from

The mark is pulled on chain from a signed forward feed. Anyone can trigger the pull. The forward
is published from the series' order book when the book is deep enough on both sides. Otherwise it
follows the carry forward from the index.

## How it can move

The chain enforces three limits on each update:

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Frequency | At most once an hour |
| Move per update | At most 5% from the previous mark |
| Distance from the index | Inside the band around the index |

The band is 10% either side of the index, and narrows linearly to 1% over the last 14 days before
expiry. It is the same band that bounds fills. See [The fill band](/futures#the-fill-band).

## Read it

[`GET /v1/markets`](/api-reference/endpoint/get) reports `mark_price` and `index_price` for each
series. [`GET /v1/positions`](/api-reference/endpoint/positions) reports `mark_price` and
`mark_updated_at` on each position. The mark is quoted in cNGN per USDC.

## At expiry

At expiry, positions close out at the settlement price, not the mark. See
[Settlement](/futures#settlement).
