---
title: "Margin math"
description: "How the MarginEngine computes initial and maintenance margin for a futures account"
---

The MarginEngine computes margin for the whole subaccount. It adds up what your collateral is worth
and subtracts what your positions require. The result is your surplus.

```text
surplus = USDC cash + cNGN value + pending VM value − Σ requirement
```

There is one surplus for initial margin (IM) and one for maintenance margin (MM).

## Position requirement

Each futures position requires a share of its notional at the mark, which moves with the price:

```text
requirement = |contracts| × contract size × mark × rate
```

| | `cNGN-30NOV26` | `EURC-30NOV26` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contract size | 1,000,000 cNGN | 1,000 EURC |
| Initial margin | 25%, plus the depeg penalty | 6% |
| Maintenance margin | 15% | 4% |

Requirements are **gross**. A long in one series does not offset a short in another.

## Collateral value

- **USDC** counts at par.
- **cNGN** counts at `cNGN held × index × 0.5`. The `0.5` is cNGN's base margin factor.
- **Pending variation margin** is USDC, and counts at par whether it is owed to you or by you.

## Depeg penalty

While cNGN trades below its depeg threshold, the MarginEngine adds a penalty to the IM rate:

```text
penalty = (threshold − cNGN price) × depeg factor
```

The penalty applies to initial margin only. The threshold and factor are set on chain.

## Worked example

You hold 2,000 USDC and no cNGN, and buy 4 contracts of `cNGN-30NOV26` with the mark at 0.00072.
Notional is 4 × 1,000,000 × 0.00072 = 2,880 USDC.

| | IM | MM |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requirement | 2,880 × 25% = 720 | 2,880 × 15% = 432 |
| Surplus | 2,000 − 720 = 1,280 | 2,000 − 432 = 1,568 |

Fees and variation margin change the cash side as you trade, and the requirement moves with the
mark. At 25% IM, leverage on `cNGN-30NOV26` is capped at 4x.

## Reducing risk

A trade that only reduces a futures position is held to maintenance margin, not initial. You can
always deleverage while your MM surplus is positive.

## Pre-trade margin check

Before each futures fill, the matcher estimates each account's IM surplus after the fill. Only fills
that add risk are checked.

- If an account comes up short, its order is held back for 30 seconds.
- After three refusals, at least a minute apart from the first, the order is cancelled with
  `cancel_reason: "insufficient_margin"`.

The estimate is deliberately generous. The MarginEngine on chain is the authority.
