---
title: "Subaccounts"
description: "Open, use and read the trading accounts that hold your collateral and positions"
---

A subaccount is a trading account under the MarginEngine. It holds your [collateral](/collateral)
and your positions. Its id is the `subaccount_id` you put on every order, deposit and withdrawal.

One subaccount trades both `cNGN-USDC` spot and the cNGN [futures](/futures). Margin covers the
whole subaccount, across every series and the cNGN it holds.

## Open a subaccount

Post a deposit to [`POST /v1/deposits`](/api-reference/endpoint/deposits) with `subaccount_id` `0`.
The venue opens the subaccount, funds it and pays the gas. The response's `subaccount_id` is your
new subaccount.

To top up a subaccount you already have, send its id instead of `0`.

## Ownership

Matching holds every subaccount and records its owner: the `owner` of the deposit that opened it.
Only the owner can deposit to it or withdraw from it, and withdrawals are paid to the owner.

A withdrawal lends the subaccount to the WithdrawalModule for the call. The subaccount stays in
Matching, so trading from it is not interrupted.

## Trade from a session key

For orders, you can sign with a session key instead of the owner key. Register it on chain with
`registerSessionKey(address toAllow, uint expiry)`. Matching then accepts that signer for all the
owner's subaccounts until `expiry`. See [Session keys](/signing#session-keys).

Deposits and withdrawals must be signed by the owner. Session keys are not supported for them.

## Use a subaccount on an order

Set `subaccount_id` in the request body and `subaccountId` in the signed `Action` to the same id.
A mismatch is refused. See [Order errors and validation](/errors#field-consistency).

## Read a subaccount

[`GET /v1/positions?subaccount_id=N`](/api-reference/endpoint/positions) returns the subaccount's
futures positions and its margin surplus, read from chain. No signature is needed, because
positions are public on chain.

## Accounts opened before the cutover

A subaccount opened before the 2026-10-04 cutover may still hold balances in the retired wrapped
assets. You can still withdraw them. See
[Create a withdrawal](/api-reference/endpoint/withdrawals#signing-the-action).
